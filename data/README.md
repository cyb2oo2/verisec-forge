# Research data

Raw corpora, processed datasets and annotation packets are retained, including the pre-existing untracked glyph-channel directory. No dataset deletion or relabeling was performed.

Small committed fixtures support portable tests. Larger datasets, predictions and checkpoints are generally ignored and must be materialized before local reanalysis.

Follow [reproducibility instructions](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md), the [annotation protocol](../docs/HUMAN_PATCH_PAIR_ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md) and the [current review](../reports/scientific_review_2026_09_07/REVIEW.md). Historical names or status flags do not establish scientific validity.
