# Current Workshop Target Shortlist

This document is the live venue search `docs/WORKSHOP_PREPRINT_TARGETING_PLAN.md`
Section 8 required and Section 11 recommended as the next step. It executes
that protocol against official CFP and conference pages only, as of this
plan's research date (mid-July 2026). It does not run experiments, train or
tune models, change paper claims, rewrite the paper, add new results, or add
new `[RESULT: ...]` anchors.

**This is a snapshot, not a standing fact.** Every field below was checked
against an official page on the date this document was written. CFPs change,
extensions happen, and pages get updated after the fact. Before acting on any
entry below — and especially before treating a "closed" entry as permanently
closed, or an "open" entry as still open — re-check the cited official URL
directly.

Sourcing rule applied throughout: only official workshop/conference pages
were used (workshop's own site, or the parent conference's official
researchr/hotcrp/sites.google.com page). Aggregator sites
(`se-deadlines.github.io`, `sec-deadlines.github.io`) were used only to
discover candidates, never as the cited source for a field value. Where a
detail could not be found on an official page, it is marked **unverified**
below, not guessed. Where a policy's wording does not clearly resolve a
question (e.g. whether it permits a concurrent preprint), it is marked
**unclear**, not assumed favorable or unfavorable.

## 1. Top 3 Realistic Targets

These are the only three candidates found, across all five required
categories, with a confirmed-open submission window as of this document's
research date:

1. **SaTML 2027** (Reykjavik, deadline Sep 29, 2026) — strong scope fit,
   confirmed-open deadline with a workable ~2.5-month runway, and the
   strongest preprint-policy precedent found (2026 edition explicitly
   permitted preprints alongside double-blind review), though the 2027
   edition's own detailed policy is not yet published.
2. **MSR 2027** (Dublin, deadline Oct 23, 2026) — confirmed-open deadline
   with a ~3-month runway and an explicit, favorable, already-published
   preprint policy, but a moderate-to-high reframing cost (empirical-SE
   research-question structure, threats-to-validity conventions) not
   required by the other two.
3. **AISec 2026** (The Hague, deadline Jul 24, 2026) — the strongest topical
   fit of the three (AI/security intersection, a new benchmark-paper
   category that suits VeriPatch-RR specifically), but the deadline is
   roughly 9 days out as of this document, which is not a realistic window
   for a properly compressed, anonymized, quality submission.

## 2. Which One to Prepare for First

**SaTML 2027.** Reasoning:

- It has the best combination of strong scope fit and a realistic
  preparation runway of the three. AISec 2026's fit is arguably tighter but
  its timeline is not realistic without rushing (see its entry above); MSR
  2027's runway is comfortable but its reframing cost is the highest of the
  three.
- Its preprint-policy precedent is the most favorable found in this entire
  search, which matters directly for the preprint-timing question below.
- **Caveat, stated plainly:** SaTML 2027's own detailed CFP (page limit,
  anonymity, preprint policy) is not yet published — only the deadline date
  and general submission-type list are confirmed. Preparation should begin
  now (compression per `docs/WORKSHOP_PREPRINT_TARGETING_PLAN.md` Section 6
  is submission-type-agnostic), but the final submission must be checked
  against SaTML 2027's actual published CFP once it appears, not against
  the 2026 precedent assumed here.

**AISec 2026 is not recommended for this cycle** given the unrealistic
timeline; it is recommended as a 2027-cycle target instead (its entry
above).

## 3. Preprint Timing Relative to Submission

**During (simultaneous), provisionally.** This updates
`docs/WORKSHOP_PREPRINT_TARGETING_PLAN.md` Section 10's default recommendation
(wait for the venue's decision unless its policy is confirmed favorable) for

but not a guarantee. Before actually posting a preprint alongside a SaTML

2027 CFP is published (expected before the September 29, 2026 deadline) and
confirm the preprint-permission language still appears. If it does not, the
prior plan's default (wait for the decision) applies instead.
