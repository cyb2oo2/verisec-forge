# Replication of the Balanced-Slice Gain

Generated by `scripts/replicate_balanced_slice_gain.py`.
Artifact: `reports/veripatch_rr_balanced_slice_replication.json`.

Tests whether the `+0.0552` both-directions-correct gain over the polarity
control reported in
[VERIPATCH_RR_BALANCED_SLICE_CONTROL.md](VERIPATCH_RR_BALANCED_SLICE_CONTROL.md)
is stable or slice-specific. Those original numbers are unchanged.

**Verdict: the gain replicates. It is not slice-specific. But the decomposition
shows it is not evidence of content-based relational understanding, and my
earlier framing of it as "the first positive result" was too generous.**

## Re-drawing with a new seed is not an independent replication

The four (gold × net-sign) cells on the clean v4 suite:

| cell | pairs |
| --- | ---: |
| gold=A, net=+ | 507 |
| gold=A, net=− | 90 |
| gold=B, net=+ | 90 |
| gold=B, net=− | 515 |

The two discordant cells are the limiting cells at 90 each, so **every balanced
draw uses them in full** and only the concordant half varies. Measured pair
overlap between seeds: `0.5833` (seed 42 vs 7) and `0.5889` (seed 42 vs 123).

Reporting reseeded draws as independent confirmations is the error withdrawn in
`docs/RESEARCH_INTEGRITY_REMEDIATION.md` (five splits overlapping at Jaccard
`0.52–0.56`). This report therefore uses three checks of increasing strength.

## Check 1 — population estimator (no subsampling)

On any sign-balanced slice the control is exactly `0.5` and the slice is half
concordant / half discordant by construction. The quantity a balanced subsample
estimates is therefore

```
0.5 * acc_concordant + 0.5 * acc_discordant - 0.5
```

evaluated over **all 1,202** non-zero-net pairs — the same estimand with no
sampling noise and maximum power.

## What the residual signal actually is

The decomposition is more informative than the aggregate.

A **pure sign rule** scores `1.0` concordant and `0.0` discordant, so its
balanced accuracy is exactly `0.5` and its delta is `0`.

A **sign rule plus symmetric noise** scores `1−ε` and `ε`, whose balanced
accuracy is *still exactly* `0.5`. Symmetric noise produces no gain.

The observed model scores `0.9227` concordant and `0.1833` discordant. It loses
`7.7` points where polarity agrees with gold and gains `18.3` points where
polarity disagrees. That asymmetry is the entire effect, and it cannot come from
noise — something other than net polarity is moving the decision, and it moves it
toward gold.

**But it is far too weak to constitute relational understanding.** The project's
own interpretation rule, from
`reports/PRIMEVUL_POLARITY_STRUCTURAL_CONTROL.md`:

> The decisive quantity is detector accuracy conditional on the semantics-free
> rule being wrong. A value at or below chance means the detector follows the
> structural shortcut into its errors rather than correcting them.

That value is **`0.1833`** — not merely below chance, but *below* the `0.2584`
that caused the Arc 1 detector claim to be withdrawn. On the pairs where polarity
misleads, the model is wrong `82%` of the time.

## Reconciling the two readings

Both statements are true and must be reported together:

1. **The gain is real and replicates.** `+0.0530` population estimate, CI
   excluding zero, 25/25 reseeded draws positive, both disjoint halves positive.
2. **It does not indicate content-based relational understanding.** Accuracy on
   anti-polarity pairs is `0.1833`, far below chance, failing the project's own
   decisive conditional test.

The correct characterisation is: *the model is a noisy polarity follower whose
errors are slightly asymmetric in its favour.* The asymmetry is genuine
information beyond the sign, but it is small, it appears only through the
antisymmetric projection (independent inference is strongly negative at
`-0.1125`), and it **vanishes entirely under re-encoding** — on the prose
rendering the same systems sit at `-0.0154` and `-0.0114`, with CIs spanning
zero and 1/25 draws positive.

A content-based signal would survive a change of surface form. This one does not,
which places it with the glyph-channel binding finding rather than against it.

## Correction to the prior report

`VERIPATCH_RR_BALANCED_SLICE_CONTROL.md` describes the `+0.0552` as "the first
positive result for a learned system in this investigation." The aggregate
statistic is positive and replicates, but that framing overstates it: the same
data show `0.1833` accuracy where the shortcut fails, which by the repository's
standing interpretation rule is a *negative* result. Read the two reports
together; the earlier numbers are unchanged and remain correct as computed.

## Claim boundary

1,202 pairs across four sources, two checkpoints, one length (1024), one seed per
checkpoint, one control family. The disjoint-half check splits one suite and is
independent in pairs but not in sources, checkpoints, or rendering pipeline. No
new training was run.
